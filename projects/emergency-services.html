Emergency Services Location Optimisation

Location-allocation and response-time modelling assessing candidate station sites for a volunteer emergency response team.

Python & MATLAB

Routing analysis

Operational research

31Incident scenarios
7+Station sites

The problem

For a volunteer rescue team, the best station is not simply the geographic centre. Responders travel from home, a response vehicle may need collecting, preparation takes time and volunteer availability changes.

Method

Historical and virtual incidents were used to test candidate station sites with travel-time data, volunteer locations and multiple response strategies.

Outcome

The project produced station-by-station response metrics, coverage comparisons and scenario results designed to support an operational location decision.
