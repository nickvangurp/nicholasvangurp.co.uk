Vice President

As Vice President of UEA Lacrosse, I helped manage the practical operation of a multi-team sports club. The work extended well beyond sporting activity and required coordination of people, schedules, fixtures, transport, committee responsibilities and changing availability throughout the academic year.

Being re-elected for a second consecutive year allowed me to build on the processes established during my first term, take greater ownership of recurring operational challenges and contribute to longer-term club organisation.

  • Coordinated training, team and match-day schedules.
  • Supported fixture and transport planning.
  • Managed changing player availability and scheduling conflicts.
  • Communicated updates across multiple teams and committee members.
  • Delegated tasks and followed up on actions.
  • Worked with university staff and external stakeholders.
  • Balanced club responsibilities alongside academic deadlines.

National-level experience

As University Lacrosse Officer, I gained experience beyond one club, communicating across university lacrosse communities and contributing to the organisation and development of the sport at a wider level.

The role strengthened my ability to consider different stakeholder requirements, communicate across organisations and approach issues from a broader strategic perspective.

Transferable value

These roles developed practical skills that transfer directly into professional environments: operations, scheduling, project coordination, administration, stakeholder management, delegation, decision-making, communication and problem-solving under changing constraints.
