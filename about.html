About

Technical thinking with an operational mindset.

I am a Computer Science graduate from the University of East Anglia with a particular interest in using technology and data to understand problems, improve processes and support better decision-making.

Comfortable moving between technical analysis, practical delivery and working with people.

My degree developed a foundation in programming, data analysis, computational problem-solving and quantitative methods. University projects required me to take problems from research and requirements through implementation, testing, analysis and presentation.

Alongside academic work, leadership roles in university and national lacrosse gave me substantial practical experience in operations, scheduling, administration, stakeholder communication and team management.

This combination means I can approach a problem from both a technical and organisational perspective: understanding the data or system, identifying constraints, communicating with stakeholders and turning analysis into a practical course of action.

I am particularly interested in graduate roles where technology, data, analytics, operations and problem-solving intersect.
